Pages

Each page of this edition holds exactly the text of one page of the manuscript. A paragraph that runs over a page break is split at the break, as in the original. “View original” opens the page in the Biblioteca Ambrosiana's viewer.

Acknowledgements

Several features of this edition are adapted from Loren Easterday's digital dissertation on Version T of La Vie de sainte Marie l'Égyptienne (University of Virginia, 2026; leeloren.github.io/dissertation): presenting the text at more than one level of editorial intervention, with each level explained on a method page; linking every page to the library's digitized image; and transcribing in plain text before encoding in TEI.
